AI-made movies and series, curated so you can just press play

Dozens of movies, series and short films made with generative AI land on YouTube every day. But they are scattered across channels, and it is hard to find episode 1 of a series. All AI-Cinema finds these titles and organizes them by genre, country and series so anyone can pick something to watch right away.

What All AI-Cinema does

  • Find – We follow 88 YouTube channels that regularly publish AI-made stories and check them for new videos every 2 hours. That is over 4,200 videos so far, with 406 added in the last 7 days.
  • Organize – Each video gets a genre and a country, and episodes of the same title are grouped into 158 series you can watch in order from episode 1.
  • Recommend – The most-watched titles each week appear in the Weekly Top 10, and every genre has its own collection. For each series we note when it started, how many episodes are out and how viewers are responding lately.

How we choose titles

  1. Only public videos whose creators allow embedding. Videos that block embedding, or become private or deleted, are checked daily and removed automatically.
  2. AI-made stories first – movies, series, shorts and animation. We do not list gameplay or simple promo clips.
  3. Videos play in the official YouTube player. Views and revenue go to the original channel, and the player links straight to the original video and channel.
  4. Rights holders can have a video removed at any time. Send the video or channel URL to the contact below and we will take it down after review.
  5. Videos marked as made for kids are shown without analytics or ad tracking.

Editor's picks

Series we recommend if you are new here. Each title page lets you watch from episode 1.

장군님 출근하십니다 씬나루 AI Studio · 12 episodes

A historical comedy about a Joseon-era general who turns up at a modern company to earn money. From episode 1 things keep going wrong, and the clash between his old-fashioned speech and office culture is where the laughs come from. Episodes are short enough for a commute.

중세에 티타늄 수트를 입음 씨네트릭스 · 16 episodes

A fantasy action series about a medieval knight who 'equips' a titanium suit. As the episode titles suggest, it plays out like a game-style power-up story — a good fit if you enjoy web novels. Best watched in order.

국밥 할머니 통쾌다방 · 6 episodes

A revenge drama about a soup-restaurant grandmother and her wealthy son-in-law. Each episode builds up an injustice and then pays it off, so it is easy to get hooked in a few minutes. New episodes have been arriving almost daily.

FALL 707: REBOOT 드라곤씨네마 · 26 episodes

An action period drama in which a special-forces captain is thrown back to the Joseon era by an explosion. With two seasons of episodes, it suits anyone looking for something to binge; the season 1 compilation is a quick way in.

콜제로 오늘도, 살아갑니다 · 15 episodes

A crime drama about a man fresh out of prison who wants a quiet life but chases the truth behind a murder he was framed for. Each episode uncovers one more clue — for viewers who prefer story over spectacle.

글로리아 판타지아 뚜라셀 · 23 episodes

A fantasy animation series that feels like an RPG adventure brought to the screen. Episodes such as 'my first fireball' follow the hero levelling up step by step — familiar and light if you like games.
