About All AI-Cinema

All AI-Cinema is a curation site that finds movies, dramas and short films made with generative AI on YouTube and gathers them by genre, country and channel. When a listed channel uploads a new video, it is added automatically. See also the FAQ.

Copyright

All videos and thumbnails belong to their YouTube channels and original creators. The Site never copies, stores or edits videos; it plays them only with YouTube's official embedded player. Views and ad revenue go to the original channel, and you can go to the original video and channel from the player.

Videos whose owners do not allow playback on other sites, and private or deleted videos, are not shown.

Takedown requests

If you are a rights holder and do not want your video to appear on the Site, please send the video or channel URL to the address below. We will remove it promptly after confirming.
