Ad placements

Main banner
A large banner on the home page between 'Now playing' and 'Latest videos'. Recommended size 1200 × 525 px (16:7).
Strip banner
A wide banner between home-page rows and inside the video list. Recommended size 1920 × 240 px (8:1). On mobile only the middle 1440 px is shown, so keep text and logos centered.
