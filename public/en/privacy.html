Privacy Policy

All AI-Cinema (the "Site") has no sign-up and does not directly collect information that identifies you, such as your name or contact details.

Cookies and ads (Google AdSense)

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Learn how Google uses information from partner sites here.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • You can block cookies in your browser settings; the Site still works.
  • Visitors from the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland see a consent message (a Google-certified consent management platform) on their first visit. No advertising or analytics cookies are set before consent, and you can change your choice at any time via the "Privacy settings" link.

Google Ads conversion measurement and remarketing

The Site uses the Google tag (Google Ads) to measure ad performance and show relevant ads. It may send visit and post-click activity to Google using cookies. You can turn off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings.

Our own banner ads and affiliate links

Banner ads on the Site are images with links and are labeled "Ad". The banners and the "AI filmmaking tools" page may include affiliate links: if you sign up or buy through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. External sites you visit are governed by their own privacy policies.

YouTube API Services

This Site uses YouTube API Services to fetch and display public video information (title, channel name, upload date, view count, thumbnail, description and whether embedding is allowed). By using this Site you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles information.

  • We do not access, collect or store visitors' personal data or YouTube account data through the YouTube API Services; only publicly available video information is used.
  • Stored video information is re-checked daily and refreshed (always within 30 days); private or deleted videos are removed.
  • While you watch a video that YouTube marks as "made for kids", the Site does not send analytics (Google Analytics) events.

YouTube player

Videos play in the YouTube embedded player (youtube-nocookie.com). The YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to improve the Site. Pages viewed, videos played, banner clicks, on-site search terms, approximate country, device, browser language and referral source are sent to Google via cookies. If Google signals is enabled, aggregated age and gender ranges may be reported according to your Google account settings; we only see aggregate numbers that cannot identify you. Share links include a tag (utm) showing which app was used. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Country lookup (automatic language)

On your first visit, to choose the display language, your browser contacts a public geolocation service (GeoJS, or country.is as a fallback) and receives only a country code (e.g., KR, US). That service processes your IP address, but the Site never receives or stores it. The country code is kept only in your browser for 7 days, and the lookup stops once you choose a language yourself.

Browser storage

Your settings and lists (Autoplay, favorites, watch history and where you stopped each video for "Continue watching") are stored only in your browser's local storage and are never sent to the Site.
