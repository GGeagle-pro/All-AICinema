Frequently asked questions

What is All AI-Cinema?

All AI-Cinema is a free curation site that gathers movies, dramas and short films made with generative AI from YouTube in one place. It currently lists 2,954 videos from 89 channels, which you can filter by genre, country and channel and play with one click.

Where can I watch AI-generated movies and AI dramas for free?

You can watch them for free, with no sign-up, on All AI-Cinema (all-aicinema.syncbrain100.com/en/). Videos play in the official YouTube player, and multi-part AI dramas can be watched in order from episode 1.

What genres and countries of AI films are there?

Genres include Fantasy, Action & Heroes, Drama, Sci-Fi, Historical & Martial Arts, Romance, with works from creators in USA, Korea, China, Japan, Spain and more.

How often are new AI movies added?

When a listed YouTube channel uploads a new video, it is added automatically within an hour.

Is it legal? Who owns the videos?

All videos belong to their original YouTube channels and creators. The site never copies videos; it only plays them in the official YouTube player, so views and revenue go to the original channel. Videos whose owners disabled embedding are removed automatically.

Which tools are used to make AI movies?

Creators generate scenes with AI video tools such as Runway, Google Veo (Flow), Sora, Kling and Higgsfield, then add voices with tools like ElevenLabs and music with tools like Suno. Tool guide: all-aicinema.syncbrain100.com/en/tools/

Can I submit my own AI film or channel?

Yes. Send your channel or video link to champkj79@gmail.com and it will be reviewed and added. Rights holders who want a video removed can use the same address.

Is the site available in other languages?

Yes: Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Spanish. The language matching your country is shown first.
