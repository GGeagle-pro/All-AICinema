Make movies with AI: recommended tools

The AI films and dramas on All AI-Cinema are made with tools like these. They're grouped in the order you'd use them: create scenes from text or images, add voices and music, then edit.

Some links on this page are affiliate links. If you sign up or buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

The AI filmmaking workflow

  1. Design characters and sets with an image tool
  2. Generate scenes (shots) with a video tool
  3. Add voices and music
  4. Edit, join shots and add captions
