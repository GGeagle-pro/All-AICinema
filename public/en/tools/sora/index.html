OpenAI Sora

OpenAI's video model that creates realistic scenes, with sound, from a text description.

Made by
OpenAI
Category
Video generation

Visit OpenAI Sora ↗

Best for

  • Short, striking scenes or trailer-style clips
  • Bringing imagined scenes to life realistically
  • Quickly testing ideas as video

Pros

  • Understands descriptions well and gets close to what you ask
  • Sound is generated too, so you can judge the mood fast
  • Easy to create and share in the app

Things to know

  • What you can use depends on your country and plan
  • Real people and copyrighted characters are blocked by policy

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Describe mood, time of day and camera lens for a cinematic result
  2. Split longer stories into scenes and join them in an editor

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
