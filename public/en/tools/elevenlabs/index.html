ElevenLabs

Creates natural AI voices and multilingual dubbing, widely used for AI drama dialogue.

Made by
ElevenLabs
Category
Voice & dubbing

Visit ElevenLabs ↗

Best for

  • Character dialogue and narration
  • Dubbing one video into several languages
  • Emotional voice acting

Pros

  • Voices sound natural and human
  • Dub the same script into other languages to reach viewers abroad
  • A large voice library makes it easy to give each character a voice

Things to know

  • Never clone someone's voice without permission
  • For monetized videos, check your plan's commercial-use terms

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Assign one voice per character and keep it to the end
  2. Note the emotion before each line (angry, whispering) for more natural acting

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
