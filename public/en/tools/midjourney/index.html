Midjourney

An image AI for high-quality concept art of characters and sets that you then feed into video tools.

Made by
Midjourney
Category
Images & concept art

Visit Midjourney ↗

Best for

  • Character sheets for your leads (front, side, full body)
  • Concept art for each location
  • Posters and thumbnails

Pros

  • Artistic, highly polished images
  • Style consistency features suit a series
  • Can also turn images into short videos

Things to know

  • Little or no free usage
  • Getting what you want takes practice with prompts

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Make a character sheet and keep using it as the reference in video tools
  2. For thumbnails, show the face large and keep the background simple

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
