HeyGen

Creates talking AI avatar videos and translated videos with matching lip movement.

Made by
HeyGen
Category
Editing & avatars

Visit HeyGen ↗

Best for

  • Scenes with a narrator or presenter speaking
  • Translating videos with lip sync
  • Series introductions and trailers

Pros

  • Lip movement and voice match well
  • Easily convert one video into many languages
  • Create speaking scenes without filming

Things to know

  • Better suited to 'talking person' videos than drama scenes
  • Never use someone's face without permission

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Great for series intros or 'previously on' recaps
  2. For foreign versions, add lip sync to scenes made with other tools

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
