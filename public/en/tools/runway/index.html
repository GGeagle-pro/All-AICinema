Runway

A leading AI video tool that turns text or images into cinematic shots.

Made by
Runway
Category
Video generation

Visit Runway ↗

Best for

  • Short shots with a cinematic look
  • Scenes that need precise camera movement
  • Restyling or refining existing footage

Pros

  • Strong image quality and lighting make a film look easy
  • Many controls for camera movement and framing
  • Generate and do light editing in one place

Things to know

  • Credits run out quickly when you generate many high-res takes
  • Keeping the same character across shots requires reference images

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Make a character sheet first and use the same reference image in every scene
  2. Keep each shot to 5–10 seconds and join them in your editor

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
