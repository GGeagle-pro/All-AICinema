Hailuo AI

MiniMax's video service, good for short scenes with expressive faces and emotion.

Made by
MiniMax
Category
Video generation

Visit Hailuo AI ↗

Best for

  • Scenes where facial expression and emotion matter
  • Short emotional close-ups
  • Short social videos

Pros

  • Rich facial expressions
  • Short scenes are quick to generate
  • Easy to get started without much commitment

Things to know

  • Limited for long scenes or complex motion
  • Characters may change between scenes, so use reference images

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Write emotions as actions, like 'holding back tears' or 'gasps in surprise'
  2. Use it for close-ups and mix in other tools for wide shots

Pricing
