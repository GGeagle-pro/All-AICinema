Google Flow (Veo)

Google's AI filmmaking tool: create scenes with the Veo video model and stitch them into a short film.

Made by
Google
Category
Video generation

Visit Google Flow (Veo) ↗

Best for

  • Scenes with sound, such as dialogue and effects
  • Linking several scenes into one short story
  • Realistic people and settings

Pros

  • Video and sound are generated together, cutting post-production
  • The scene-building workflow is designed for filmmaking
  • Realistic look with natural physical motion

Things to know

  • Available features depend on your Google AI plan and country
  • Generation limits mean you need to plan your shots

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Write each scene as who, where, what and how the camera moves
  2. Keep dialogue shots short so lips and sound stay in sync

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
