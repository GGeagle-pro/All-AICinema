CapCut

A free editor for cutting and joining scenes and adding captions and effects, with built-in AI features.

Made by
ByteDance
Category
Editing & avatars

Visit CapCut ↗

Best for

  • Joining AI-generated shots into one video
  • Automatic captions
  • Vertical edits for Shorts and Reels

Pros

  • Most editing features are free
  • Plenty of auto captions, sound effects and transitions
  • Works on both phone and PC

Things to know

  • Some advanced features and effects are paid
  • Check the terms of in-app music and effects

Tips for AI filmmaking

  1. Always add captions – many people watch without sound
  2. Use the same opening and ending in every episode to build a series identity

Pricing

Most tools offer a free trial or free credits, while high resolution, longer clips and commercial use often require a paid plan. Prices and terms change often, so check the official site for the latest details.
