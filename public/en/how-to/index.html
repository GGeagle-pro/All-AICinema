How to make an AI movie or series: 7 steps from idea to upload

We organize thousands of AI-made titles every day at All AI-Cinema, so we gathered how the most-watched ones are made. No camera or actors needed — one computer is enough to start.

  1. Decide the story (concept and script)
  2. Create character and background images
  3. Turn images into video shots
  4. Add voices and dubbing
  5. Add music and sound effects
  6. Edit and add subtitles
  7. Upload to YouTube

1Decide the story (concept and script)

Start with a series of 1–3 minute episodes. Show the situation (who, what problem) in the first 5 seconds and end each episode on a moment that makes people want the next one. Popular titles on the site are often time-slip, revenge, or 'ordinary person gains a special power' stories. Asking an AI chatbot to outline the plot and scene-by-scene script saves time.

2Create character and background images

The hardest part of an AI film is keeping the same character looking the same in every shot. Make a 'character sheet' (front, side, full body) for each lead first, and feed the same reference image into every scene. Fixing one image per location also keeps scenes consistent.

3Turn images into video shots

Add your image and describe the motion — 'slow push-in', 'she turns her head' — to get a 5–10 second shot. Generate the same shot 3–4 times and keep the most natural one. Each tool has its strengths (realistic people, action, animated looks), so many creators mix them by scene.

Recommended tools Runway ↗Kling AI ↗Google Flow (Veo) ↗Hailuo AI ↗Luma Dream Machine ↗Seedance (ByteDance) ↗

4Add voices and dubbing

Pick one voice per character and keep it to the end. Writing the emotion with each line (angry, whispering) makes it sound more natural. Dubbing the same script into other languages lets you reach viewers in other countries.

Recommended tools ElevenLabs ↗

5Add music and sound effects

Describe the mood (tense, sad, comic) to generate background music. If the video will earn money, check that your plan allows commercial use.

Recommended tools Suno ↗

6Edit and add subtitles

Join the shots, add dialogue subtitles and sound effects between scenes. Subtitles are almost essential because many people watch with the sound off. For matching lip movement to dialogue, use an avatar or lip-sync tool.

Recommended tools CapCut ↗HeyGen ↗

7Upload to YouTube

Put the episode number in the title ([Ep 1], [Ep 2]) and build a series playlist. Realistic AI video must be marked as 'altered or synthetic content' when uploading. Keep 'Allow embedding' on so sites like All AI-Cinema can feature it.

Before you start

  • Most tools offer a free trial or free credits. Finish a one-minute episode 1 before choosing a paid plan.
  • Do not use the faces or voices of real people (celebrities, politicians) or existing film and comic characters. It can lead to copyright and likeness issues and channel strikes.
  • Commercial-use terms differ by tool. Check each tool's terms before monetizing.
